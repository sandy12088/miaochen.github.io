Hello! I am Miao Chen, an M.Sc. student in Theoretical Physics at Beijing Normal University under the supervision of Prof. Yu-Han Ma. I received my B.Sc. in Physics from Jiangxi Normal University in 2024.

My research focuses on non-equilibrium statistical physics, especially dynamic hysteresis, phase transitions, and information thermodynamics. I aim to understand how driven many-body systems respond across time and length scales, and how universal constraints emerge from microscopic dynamics.
